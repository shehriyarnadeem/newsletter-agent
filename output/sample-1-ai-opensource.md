### [A case study of evaluating AI agents on a neuroscience data-to-discovery pipeline](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07718)

## open-source tooling

### [Syll: Open-Source Personal Automation with Cross-Surface Execution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07594)
*ArXiv CS.AI*

Syll is an open-source, self-hosted multimodal agent harness designed to let personal AI agents work across APIs, shells, web surfaces, and desktop GUIs. It emphasizes user teaching through direct demonstration, auditability through logs and approval checkpoints, and editable local artifacts for memory, skills, routines, and governance.
